# Creatures after Calamity

Creatures After Calamity is an unsettling monster battler in which your animals remember how you treat them. Build a team and outsmart your opponents before your creatures turn on you.

![Creatures After Calamity Battle](/images/cac-battle.gif)

Concepted as part of the NYU Game Center Incubator, Creatures after Calamity is a turn-based battling strategy game, where you traverse a post-apocalyptic urban landscape, and experience a deconstructive branching storyline based on your choices. CAC features an integrated single-player and online-multiplayer game mode, with run-based roguelike gameplay and innovative ghost-based asynchronous and real-time battles.

![Creatures After Calamity Cutscene](/images/cac-movie-1.webm)

Developed by our two-person studio Never Done Games, its slated for release in 2027.
