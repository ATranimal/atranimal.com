# The Archipelago

[Link to steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2169730/The_Archipelago/)

The Archipelago is a visual novel about brewing tea. The teas you choose determine who you encounter in the midst of a vibrant and changing world.

[The Archipelago Trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ov6mHXv_N7o)

Developed as part of the Brainmind Residency run by HBomberguy, the Archipelago was developed by our two person studio Never Done Games in 6 months. It was developed in Unity, and released for free on Steam in 2022. As of 2026, it has amassed >100 reviews and 85000 installs.
