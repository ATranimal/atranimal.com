# One More Multiverse

[Link to game](https://playmultiverse.com)

One More Multiverse is an online virtual tabletop to play, share, and stream your next tabletop roleplaying game. With a massive and ever-growing pixel art library collection, in OMM create a level that changes as quickly as your story.

[One More Multiverse Trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9lOjjlQgj4Q)

I was one of the first engineers on the team, working on real-timne multiplayer features, UGC support, engine development, built on top of a Three.JS rendering engine, React UI framework, and Typescript gameplay engine running on Firestore. We reached 20+ million views on Tiktok, 38k members on our discord, 300k registered users, with a peak of 20k daily active users.

![OMM Gameplay](/images/omm-gif.gif)

Unfortunately, we were not able to sustain the team and transitioned the game to a Patreon model in 2024.
